# Expanded Relic Trial Guides — Draft

These sections belong after each relic’s portal-unlock instructions. This document contains trial descriptions, not the full portal-opening quests.

## Shared disclaimer — display once above the list

Check off a relic only after completing its trial and receiving the unlock. Opening its portal does not count as completion.

Follow the on-screen trial instructions and timers. Enemy compositions can vary; HVT examples are not guaranteed spawn lists. Recommendations are strategies, not extra requirements. Unconfirmed details are explicitly identified. A relic’s equipped effect is not necessarily its trial modifier.

# Ashes of the Damned

## 1. Lawyer’s Pen
- Objective: Complete four waves of Shock Mimics, including HVT encounters.
- Modifier: Shock Mimics replace normal zombie opposition.
- Preparation: Bring an upgraded weapon for sustained single-target damage and full ammunition.
- Strategy: Keep distance from grabs, leave an escape route, and separate HVT Mimics from the group.
- Warning: Electrical bursts can destroy deployed equipment; do not make equipment your only survival or damage plan.

## 2. Dragon Wings
- Objective: Complete four waves.
- Modifier: Picking up power-ups damages you, and the power-ups do not activate.
- Documented sequence: Ordinary enemy waves first and third; HVT encounters second and fourth.
- Preparation: Refill ammunition and armor; do not rely on collecting Max Ammo.
- Strategy: Fight in open space, track dropped power-ups, and route around them.
- Warning: Treat every power-up as a hazard during this trial.

## 3. Teddy Bear
- Objective: Complete four combat waves.
- Modifier: Each weapon shot costs 100 Essence.
- Preparation: Bring a substantial Essence reserve and an upgraded, ammunition-efficient weapon.
- Strategy: Prioritize accurate shots; group enemies before attacks that hit multiple targets.
- Warning: Rapid fire drains Essence quickly.
- Unverified: Exact behavior at zero Essence. Do not promise free firing or automatic failure at that point.

## 4. Vril Sphere
- Objective: Complete five waves at Zarya Cosmodrome, including HVT encounters.
- Modifier: Purchases are disabled.
- Preparation: Complete weapon upgrades and obtain ammunition, armor, equipment, and scorestreaks before activation.
- Strategy: Use ordinary weapons for routine enemies; save limited-use tools for HVTs.
- Source caveat: The detailed wiki describes no purchases, while an older community summary describes the restriction less consistently. Do not label it merely a no-perks trial.

## 5. Samantha’s Drawing
- Objective: Complete five waves of zombies and HVTs.
- Modifier: Ammo caches and Max Ammo power-ups are disabled.
- Preparation: Fill ammunition reserves and bring more than one useful damage option.
- Strategy: Use efficient attacks for ordinary enemies and reserve high-damage ammunition for HVTs.
- Warning: Do not assume every ammunition-related GobbleGum bypasses the restriction.

## 6. Focusing Stone
- Objective: Complete the combat trial after surrendering your Essence temporarily.
- Modifier: Existing Essence is taken away; you may earn new Essence while fighting.
- Preparation: Purchase essential upgrades and supplies before entry.
- Strategy: Start with owned equipment and use newly earned Essence for available purchases.
- Completion: The wiki reports that surrendered Essence returns after success.
- Unverified: Exact wave count.

## 7. Bus
- Objective: Clear the challenge using zombies to kill other zombies.
- Modifier: Direct player damage cannot eliminate enemies; zombie-on-zombie damage is required.
- Preparation: Equip Brain Rot and a suitable weapon to apply it.
- Strategy: Convert a zombie, then bring unconverted enemies into its reach without trapping yourself.
- Warning: Firing applies Brain Rot; it does not bypass the kill restriction.
- Unverified: Exact wave count and HVT-specific conversion interactions.

## 8. Dragon
- Objective: Clear the trial with explosive damage.
- Modifier: Only explosive kills qualify; the Necrofluid Gauntlet’s explosive attacks are documented as valid.
- Preparation: Bring an upgraded explosive damage source, ammunition, and protection from self-inflicted explosions where available.
- Strategy: Group enemies and preserve ammunition for tougher targets.
- Warning: Highly upgraded conventional bullets do not bypass the restriction.
- Unverified: Exact wave count.

## 9. Blood Vials
- Objective: Complete the combat trial with reduced damage.
- Modifier: Player damage is halved.
- Preparation: Upgrade weapons and stock ammunition for longer engagements.
- Strategy: Target weak points and finish dangerous enemies before spreading damage.
- Warning: Expect longer kill times and keep more distance than usual.
- Unverified: Exact wave count.

# Astra Malorum

## 10. Gong
- Objective: Complete four waves of zombies and HVTs.
- Modifier: Only electrical damage works. The wiki reports that weapons equipped with Dead Wire qualify.
- Preparation: Equip Dead Wire and have Tesla Storm available.
- Strategy: Use an electrically qualified weapon as reliable damage instead of relying entirely on Field Upgrade uptime.
- Warning: Do not enter with only a non-electrical upgraded weapon.

## 11. Seed
- Objective: Complete four waves using the supplied Pack-a-Punch I pistol.
- Modifier: You are restricted to the pistol and cannot raise its Pack-a-Punch level.
- Documented sequence: Zombies and Mimics first and third; HVT Mimics second and fourth.
- Preparation: Be ready to fight with a pistol rather than your normal primary weapon.
- Strategy: Aim for weak points, keep distance, and avoid emptying a magazine while cornered.
- Exception: The wiki reports that the pistol’s rarity can still be raised at the Arsenal.

## 12. Spider Fang
- Objective: Complete five waves without perks.
- Modifier: Perks are removed or disabled; other utilities remain available.
- Enemies: Reported encounters include Mimics, Uber Klaus, and O.S.C.A.R., including HVT versions.
- Preparation: Bring upgraded weapons and equipment. Do not rely on perk-enhanced movement, reload speed, health, or explosive protection.
- Strategy: Keep escape routes open. The wiki recommends the Apogee Annihilator for tougher targets.
- Warning: Your usual perk-based timing and survivability do not apply.

## 13. Matryoshka Doll
- Objective: Eliminate enemies with Field Upgrade damage.
- Modifier: Ordinary weapon damage does not satisfy the kill restriction.
- Preparation: Choose a damaging Field Upgrade and enter with it charged.
- Strategy: Group enemies before activation to maximize qualifying kills.
- Warning: A utility-only or defensive Field Upgrade may leave you without a practical killing method.
- Unverified: Wave count, charge supply during the challenge, and qualifying individual upgrades or secondary damage interactions.

## 14. Golden Spork
- Objective: Clear enemies while firing from the hip.
- Modifier: Enemies can only be damaged while you are hip-firing.
- Preparation: Use a weapon effective and controllable without aiming down sights.
- Strategy: Stay within useful hip-fire range while preserving room to retreat.
- Warning: Do not reflexively aim down sights against an HVT.
- Unverified: Wave count and whether non-firearm damage qualifies.

## 15. Civil Protector Head
- Objective: Complete six waves of zombies and HVTs.
- Modifier: Zombies sprint at maximum speed.
- Preparation: Bring crowd control, upgraded weapons, and a reliable emergency escape option.
- Strategy: Follow a planned movement route; clear space ahead before turning to fire. Use crowd control before being surrounded.
- Warning: Slow training patterns are less forgiving against immediate, fast-moving threats.

# Paradox Junction

## 16. Rocket
- Objective: Eliminate enemies by exploiting elemental weaknesses.
- Modifier: Only the element an enemy is weak against deals qualifying damage.
- Preparation: Arrange access to multiple elemental damage types.
- Strategy: Identify the enemy, apply the matching element, and confirm damage before committing ammunition.
- Warning: One powerful element is not a substitute for the required matchup.
- Unverified: Wave count, complete enemy-to-element table, and which ammo-mod or alternate elemental applications qualify.

## 17. Summoning Key
- Objective: Kill required enemies before the timer expires.
- Modifier: Timed elimination, not unrestricted survival.
- Preparation: Full ammunition and strong burst damage for high-health targets.
- Strategy: Keep checking the timer; avoid leaving a difficult target until the final seconds.
- Failure: Required enemies remain when the timer expires.
- Unverified: Exact time limits, wave count, and fixed enemy composition.

## 18. Mangler Helmet
- Objective: Survive six waves.
- Modifiers: Reduced health and no armor.
- Preparation: Upgraded weapons, crowd control, and an emergency escape option. The community guide recommends a Ray Gun Mark II or Sundergat.
- Strategy: Fight at range and reposition early; control enemies before contact.
- Warning: Do not rely on taking a hit to force your way through a group.
- Unverified: Exact health cap and dependable wave-by-wave enemy list.

# Totenreich

## 19. Power Switch
- Objective: Eliminate enemies using traps.
- Modifier: Only trap kills count.
- Preparation: Learn a safe route through traps and reserve activation resources.
- Strategy: Gather enemies, activate a trap, and lead them through it. Reposition during cooldowns.
- Warning: Weapon damage is not a replacement for trap kills.
- Unverified: Wave count and exceptions for environmental damage sources.

## 20. Wrestling Belt
- Objective: Complete four waves, including HVT encounters.
- Modifier: All weapons are removed at the end of each wave.
- Preparation: Reserve enough Essence for repeated weapon purchases and upgrades.
- Strategy:
  1. Finish the opening wave with your current weapons.
  2. Obtain replacements when those weapons disappear.
  3. Upgrade the replacement loadout as needed.
  4. Repeat until all four waves are cleared.
- Warning: Do not spend your entire reserve on the opening loadout; several replacement loadouts must be funded.

## 21. Stuffed Elephant
- Objective: Find cursed eggs and kill the HVT before time expires.
- Structure: Timed search-and-kill challenge.
- Preparation: Learn the area and bring strong HVT damage.
- Strategy: Search immediately, complete the egg objective, and leave enough time for the HVT. Avoid incidental fights unless they obstruct progress.
- Failure: Time expires before the required objectives are complete.
- Unverified: Egg count, exact timer, spawn pool, and number of timed stages.

## 22. Dancing Arnie
- Objective: Clear enemies using melee attacks.
- Modifier: Only melee kills satisfy the challenge.
- Preparation: Upgrade the melee weapon and prepare a way to create space.
- Strategy: Keep enemies in front, attack near the edge of their reach, and break contact when the group becomes dense.
- Warning: Do not assume the Jotunn Star’s ranged attack qualifies because the weapon is involved in portal setup.
- Unverified: Wave count and qualifying special melee or weapon-specific attacks.

## 23. Agarthan Device
- Objective: Eliminate enemies indoors.
- Modifier: Enemies can only be killed inside.
- Preparation: Choose an indoor position with manageable entrances and an escape route.
- Strategy: Let targets enter before firing and avoid allowing the entire group in at once.
- Warning: The community summary does not define whether the indoor check applies to player, enemy, or both. Until confirmed, keep both indoors.
- Unverified: Wave count and qualifying indoor boundaries.

## 24. Music Box
- Objective: Activate and survive six successive holdouts.
- Structure: Find the purple beam, travel to Mister Peeks, interact, and survive the restricted-area lockdown.
- Transition timer: The detailed guide gives 30 seconds to activate each next holdout. Missing the window begins damaging you; it is not described as instant failure.
- Escalation: Later stages include Necropincers and smaller safe areas.
- Preparation: Crowd control, close-range damage, and resources for multiple locations.
- Strategy: Find the next beam immediately after each holdout. Move before reorganizing or looting.
- Unverified: The complete ordered six-location route.

# Kowakujo

## 25. Gramophone
- Objective: Complete four waves with sniper-rifle damage.
- Modifier: Enemies only take damage from sniper rifles.
- Documented sequence: Ordinary waves first and third; HVT Oni encounters second and fourth.
- Preparation: Buy the XR-3 Ion near Pack-a-Punch and upgrade it. Pack-a-Punch III is recommended, not an entry requirement.
- Strategy: Use Maneki-Bomb bubbles for safe firing positions, aim carefully, and reposition when protection ends.
- Warning: The scorestreak-only trial is Film Reel, not Gramophone.

## 26. Druid Stone
- Objective: Complete four waves while maintaining overheal.
- Modifier: You deal damage only while overhealed.
- Documented sequence: Ordinary waves first and third; HVT encounters second and fourth.
- Preparation: Bring the Nekomancer and upgraded Maneki-Bombs. Obtain overheal from a cherry blossom tree before entry.
- Strategy:
  1. Melee a cherry blossom tree to activate it.
  2. Stay beneath it to replenish overheal.
  3. Use charged Nekomancer shots while overhealed.
  4. Against HVTs, use a protective bubble and strong damage while maintaining overheal.
- Warning: If damage stops registering, check overheal first. Sources give inconsistent numerical thresholds; do not publish one universal HP value.

## 27. Valkyrie Helmet
- Objective: Complete five waves.
- Modifier: Reloading triggers a damaging explosion.
- Documented sequence: HVT encounters in waves two and five.
- Preparation: Upgraded Nekomancer, Maneki-Bombs, and a War Machine or another useful HVT damage source.
- Strategy:
  1. Group ordinary enemies and use charged Nekomancer shots to reduce reload frequency.
  2. Reload with adequate health and room around you.
  3. Let health recover before another risky reload.
  4. Use bubbles and reserve stronger limited-use damage for HVTs.
- Additional option: The walkthrough reports Ghostly Rifleman trap kills count.
- Warning: Reload damage varies between weapons and demonstrations; do not publish a fixed universal damage number.

## 28. Film Reel
- Objective: Complete five timed waves with each wave’s assigned scorestreak.
- Modifier: Only the designated scorestreak deals qualifying damage.
- Pool: ARC-XD, LDBR, HKDs, D.A.W.G., and Ion Core, in randomized order.
- Supply: One assigned streak is provided free at each wave’s start; craft additional copies if needed.
- Preparation: The video recommends at least 15,000 salvage; more gives room for mistakes.
- Strategy by streak:
  - ARC-XD: Move slowly near the horde so its pulses work; avoid crashing directly into enemies and ending it early.
  - LDBR: Mark an area and lure enemies through the bombardment.
  - HKDs: Gather enemies into a useful target concentration.
  - D.A.W.G.: Deploy where it can immediately engage the group; the walkthrough recommends Insta-Kill support.
  - Ion Core: Place in a busy area and keep targets within its effective zone.
- Failure: Insufficient qualifying kills before the wave timer expires.
- Retry: The video reports the portal returns after additional rounds; an exact one-round cooldown is not confirmed.

## 29. Dragon Egg
- Objective: Complete a six-stage challenge, ending in a timed boss fight.
- Preparation: Strong Nekomancer and boss-damage weapon, upgraded armor, crowd control, and an emergency Field Upgrade. Pack-a-Punch IV requires Cursed Tier III; that recommendation does not invalidate Tier II entry.

### Stage 1 — Assault defense
Defend the marked ward for two minutes against high-round enemies. Use charged Nekomancer shots to control approaches. Follow the marked location rather than assuming one fixed ward.

### Stage 2 — HVT Abominations
Go to Gatehouse and kill two HVT Abominations. The walkthrough recommends firing a War Machine into exposed mouths during beam attacks. Demonstrated very fast kills are not guaranteed damage.

### Stage 3 — Mister Peeks escort
Escort Mister Peeks from Central Courtyard toward the keep along the pink-flame route. Stay close enough to maintain progress and clear Doppelghasts. Save escape or crowd-control tools for the denser attack near Tenshu Entrance.

### Stage 4 — Electrified katana
Parry Oni electrical attacks to charge the supplied sword, then damage the Oni with the electrified sword. Ordinary attacks do not replace the charged attack. Practice parries beforehand.

### Stage 5 — Fissure survival and elimination
Fight Doppelghasts in Central Courtyard while the fissure applies environmental pressure. Kill promptly and avoid their explosions; drawn-out fighting compounds the pressure.

### Stage 6 — Timed boss
Defeat the boss within ten minutes. Clear required Oni and complete flag mechanics promptly to expose damage opportunities, then focus the boss. Expect high-round enemies and more dangerous attacks than a normal low-round fight.

- Transition warning: The walkthrough gives 30-second travel windows between several stages. Find the next Mister Peeks immediately instead of treating transitions as unlimited preparation time.

## 30. Mannequin Turret
- Objective: Complete six waves.
- Modifier: Passive health regeneration is disabled. Full healing occurs at each wave’s end.
- Other healing: The written guide reports Stims and Light Mend still work.
- Documented sequence: HVT Oni waves third and sixth, with a larger final group.
- Preparation: Upgraded Nekomancer, reliable active healing, and strong HVT damage.
- Strategy:
  1. Control regular enemies with charged shots.
  2. Avoid damage rather than waiting for passive regeneration.
  3. Use active healing during a wave when necessary.
  4. Save burst damage for HVT encounters.
- Warning: Escaping enemies and waiting does not restore passive health regeneration.
